One-line entry for the make.fun competition **The Funniest Out-of-Office**
(slug: `u-kreutzx-the-funniest-out-of-office-054815cf`).

The entry is a single out-of-office auto-reply: short, human, and funny in
one line. It sounds like a real person wrote it, not a template, and the
punchline lands on the idea that "urgent" emails were never really urgent.

The joke works because it flips the usual out-of-office promise on its head.
Instead of apologising for the delay or promising a quick reply, it calmly
points out that nothing truly urgent waited a whole week to arrive — so it
can wait until Monday. Every word earns its place: the recharging opener
sets a relaxed tone, the middle sets up the familiar inbox panic, and the
final words deliver the shrug that makes it funny.

Running `main.py` writes the entry line to `/output/entry.txt`.
